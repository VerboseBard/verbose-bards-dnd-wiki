Seventh Age · Kex Shipyard

Tester guide

Thank you for testing. The Kex Shipyard is the party's repair board for their crashed starship, the Kex: what it needs, who gave what, and how its repairs move along day by day. This page tells you what to try and how to report what you find. It takes 15 to 30 minutes.

Open the shipyard

Before you start

  • Use any device in a normal browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox), not the browser built into Discord or Messenger. A phone and a computer are both worth trying.
  • If you opened the site before, refresh it first so you have the latest version: Ctrl+F5 on a computer, or pull down to reload on a phone. The build number is under Office → About.
  • Nothing you do affects anyone else. Everything you press is saved on your device only. The game master's official record changes only when the game master publishes it.
  • Turn sound on when asked, if you can: the tutorials are spoken by the ship's AI.
  • On a phone, the top buttons are icons: the curved arrow is Undo, the speaker is Sound, the question mark is Tutorial and the disk is Office. The tab bar slides sideways to reach Upgrades, Hold, Codex and Log.
  • To start over: open Office and press Reset to official record. To see a tutorial again, use Tutorial.

Try these, in order

  1. The message packet. On your first visit it starts by itself: the ship's AI explains power, fuel and repairs. Play it to the end.
  2. The refit drill. At the end of the packet, choose Next: the refit drill. You can also open it later from Tutorial, or from How upgrades work on the Upgrades page. It shows how to supply, build and unlock systems, and how days pass.
  3. Explore. Open every tab at the top. On Kex, switch decks, tap rooms and open a room's map full screen.
  4. Do a repair yourself. Open the hangar job, feed it power from the ship's reserve and start the work. (Days only pass when the game master moves them on, so the work won't finish by itself.)
  5. Unlock something. On Upgrades, tap any violet "encrypted" card that says tap to decode and start decoding it. Cards that say Kubix "cannot reach yet" open later in the story.
  6. Plan a shopping trip. Add a few things to the shopping list from a project or the Hold page, change the amounts, then copy the list.
  7. Look things up. Search the Codex for a magic item you know.
  8. Check the record. Read the Log, then use the undo arrow in the top bar to take back your last change.

What to look for

  • Anything that doesn't work, shows an error, or gets stuck.
  • Anything confusing: you didn't know what to press next, or a label didn't mean what you expected.
  • Anything where the words disagree with the screen, such as a number or a button name.
  • Anything hard to read or tap on a phone, or that runs off the side of the screen.
  • Sound problems: the voice not playing, playing over itself, or the wrong line.

How to report a problem

  1. At the bottom of any page (or in Office), press Report a problem.
  2. Write what you did, what happened, and what you expected.
  3. Press Copy report and send it to the game master however your group chats. The report includes your page, device and the ship's last few log lines, so the game master can see what you saw. A screenshot helps too.

The report is never sent anywhere by the site. If copying doesn't work on your device, use Save as a file instead.

Good to know

  • Learn and Recycle in the Codex destroy an item, so they ask you to confirm. Only use them for items the party really has.
  • Nothing can be bought on the site. Purchases happen in play, and the game master records them. You can plan purchases with the shopping list.
  • Some systems are encrypted on purpose: they open as the story goes on, by repairs or by decoding.
  • All the numbers are draft rules for the campaign and may change between sessions.
  • On an iPhone, the mute switch silences the small interface sounds but not the AI's voice. That's normal.
