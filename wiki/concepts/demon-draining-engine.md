# Demon-Draining Engine

The demon-draining engine is an ancient ritual installation inside the complex beneath the [Dead Lands](../places/dead-lands.md), discovered at the end of [Session 11](../sessions/session-11.md).

## Function

The chamber contains a vast altar and chains designed to drain a demon of divine power it had absorbed and convert that power into a form mortals could use. [Alistair Hooley](../people/alistair-hooley.md) sees thousands of souls layered over the room and ancient elven ritual writing across the walls and floor.

## State on Discovery

The engine's chains were broken only one or two weeks before the coalition arrived in [Session 11](../sessions/session-11.md). The identity and purpose of whoever broke them remain unknown. At that time, the room contains or attracts a roughly forty-foot demon as well as lesser fiends, and [the Justicar](../people/the-justicar.md) detects a second demon elsewhere in the complex.

## Alistair's Tether

When Alistair reads the chamber in Session 11, the knowledge locks into him and costs one point of [sanity](sanity.md). A cord forms between his chest and the greater demon. He wins an initial contest of wills, and both bleed, but the link remains intact at the end of that session.

In [Session 12](../sessions/session-12.md), Alistair's physical body is motionless while his awareness confronts the demon through the connection. He partially loosens the tether and later sends damaging power back through it. Those exchanges demonstrate a way to hurt this demon within the encounter; they do not establish general control over demons or a settled set of tether abilities.

## Session 12 Battle and Aftermath

Alistair sees lesser creatures crossing into the chamber through its magical boundaries. By drawing a chalk unicorn into the ancient wall inscriptions, he closes one reinforcement conduit. The backlash strengthens his attachment to the demon, and other reinforcements continue arriving. The demon also gains an opportunity to use a different lair action, attempting a fireball that the Justicar counterspells.

The coalition defeats the chamber's defenders. The Justicar's radiant line attack disintegrates two lesser demons and the greater demon, and Alistair returns to ordinary perception. The tether shrinks to about finger width instead of disappearing; [Rurik Valdren](../people/rurik-valdren.md) later reveals that it is still present. Its destination and long-term effects remain unknown.

The chamber battle ends in round four. Cleansing the remaining taint is still a separate task after the victory. The battle does not resolve who broke the engine's chains or establish that the valley's blood curse has been lifted.
