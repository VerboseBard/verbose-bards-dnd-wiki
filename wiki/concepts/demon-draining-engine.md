# Demon-Draining Engine

The demon-draining engine is an ancient ritual installation inside the complex beneath the [Dead Lands](../places/dead-lands.md), discovered at the end of [Session 11](../sessions/session-11.md).

## Function

The chamber contains a vast altar and chains designed to drain a demon of divine power it had absorbed and convert that power into a form mortals could use. [Alistair Hooley](../people/alistair-hooley.md) sees thousands of souls layered over the room and ancient elven ritual writing across the walls and floor.

## Current State

The engine's chains were broken only one or two weeks before the coalition arrived. The identity and purpose of whoever broke them are unknown. The room contains or attracts a roughly forty-foot demon as well as lesser fiends, and the Justicar detects a second demon elsewhere in the complex.

## Alistair's Tether

When Alistair reads the chamber, the knowledge locks into him and costs one point of [sanity](sanity.md). A cord forms between his chest and the greater demon. He wins an initial contest of wills, and both bleed, but he cannot sever the link before the recording ends. Whether the tether transfers damage, permits control, or makes him part of the engine remains unresolved.
