# Dragon Guardians and Succession

The dragon guardians were not merely powerful creatures. A Third-Age rewriting of reality tied dragon lives to the stability of the world, the divine barrier, and the laws governing ascension.

## Third-Age Binding

After [the First Dragon](../people/first-dragon.md)'s failed destruction and fifty-year rampage created the [Dead Lands](../places/dead-lands.md), an unknown person harnessed the resulting death energy to rewrite the laws of reality.

The identity of the rewriter is unknown. It is suspected that [the Weaver](the-weaver.md) manipulated this person or showed them how to perform the rewrite, but this is not confirmed.

The new law:

- tied the lives of dragons to the defense and stability of reality;
- reinforced the divine barrier that limited direct intervention by gods;
- restricted true ascension to existing gods, their children, or beings already possessing equivalent power;
- established one active guardian place for each draconic race or color; and
- held replacement eggs in stasis until a corresponding dragon died.

## Eggs and Weakness in the Barrier

A dragon's death permitted a replacement egg to hatch, but did not guarantee it. The correct conditions still had to exist. If no suitable egg could hatch, that place in the guardian structure remained empty and reality's defenses stayed weakened until a successor emerged.

This meant that dragon extinction was not merely a loss of creatures. Every missing guardian damaged the structure holding the world and divine boundary together.

## Tiamat and Her Brother

[Tiamat](../people/tiamat.md), one of the First Dragon's two principal children, became the Mother of Dragons. Her brother, remembered in later traditions as Bahamut, became their protector.

Their roles stood above the individual guardians associated with the established draconic races and colors. They were the two principal children of the First Dragon in addition to that race-and-color succession.

## The Breaking

[Lucius](../people/lucius.md) exploited this law at the end of the [Fifth Age](fifth-age.md). He recruited lesser beings seeking ascension, manipulated great heroes, and arranged the killing of the dragon guardians. Removing the dragon anchors weakened the Divine Gate and the world's structural defenses until [the Breaking](the-breaking.md) became possible.
