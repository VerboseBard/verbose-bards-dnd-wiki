# Fourth Age

## Description

The Fourth Age is now one of the better-witnessed ancient ages in the campaign, thanks to the Sessions 8–9 lore recoveries: a living Fourth-Age survivor ([Bartholomew Hildebrant](../people/bartholomew-hildebrant.md)), a living Fourth-Age commander ([Rurik Valdren](../people/rurik-valdren.md)), the [Dead Lands Ghost Druid](../people/dead-lands-ghost-druid.md), and the temple's own mosaics and guardians.

Its defining conflict: the age-spanning war against [Lolth](../people/lolth.md) and her cult, ending in the coordinated strikes that also brought down [the First Dragon](../people/first-dragon.md).

## The Dwarven Hegemony

The dwarves were the dominant power of the age — in Rurik's words, "the America of the world" — and by its end (and through the Fifth) they had effectively conquered it. The engine of that dominance was discipline forged on [the Dragon Watch](../places/dragon-watch.md): the penance-wall against the undead wastes their summoned dragon had made, held for roughly 4,500 years, with every dwarf serving a rotation out of training. The order that manned it was [the Sentinels of the Veil](../factions/sentinels-of-the-veil.md); its elite **Demon Hunters** and **Slayer Teams** (whose members bore the title **Warden**) handled demons, devils, and the worst of the supernatural.

Fourth-Age dwarven arcane arts included **full-body spellcasting tattoos** — Bartholomew's banishment tattoo is one; the Radiant Citadel's modern tattoo-artificers are described as poor imitations of that art.

## Lolth's Rise and Fall

See [Lolth's Rise and the Great Crusade](lolths-rise-and-the-great-crusade.md) for the dedicated conflict history and the incomplete thirteen-temple ledger.

Assembled from the druid's account, the temple mosaics, and Rurik's history:

- A luxury-born drow rejected the elven conclave's beauty as a lie, embraced the doctrine that *"peace is a lie — pain is the truest form of existence,"* found her model in the spider, and bred, studied, and interbred with them.
- She allied with [the First Dragon](../people/first-dragon.md) — with, per the mosaics ⚠, a betrayer possessing *foresight* ([the Weaver](the-weaver.md)) behind the arrangement — and **killed a god and stole its power: the first mortal deicide.** Other divinities died in her era, among them a goddess of nature and [Hestia](../people/hestia.md) (see below). [Big Shad](big-shad.md), the elven divinity sometimes grouped with these losses in later testimony, had instead willingly sacrificed himself during the Third-Age famine.
- With stolen divine power she walked into the halls of all the elves — sacred ground where violence was impossible — **betrayed them, seized the holy valley (the Veil), drove the other elves out, and corrupted its temples** into [Ssar'Velyn Temple](../places/ssar-velyn-temple.md)'s cult complex.
- **The Great Crusade** answered the deicide and drove her from the valley. Many drow fought *against* her.
- When her cult later turned to **summoning demons and devils**, the gods pressed for her destruction: **coordinated, simultaneous strikes on all her places of power**. The [Ssar'Velyn Temple](../places/ssar-velyn-temple.md) assault — ten mixed teams, roughly forty soldiers, Bartholomew's among them — was one arm of that operation. The cult was mostly wiped out; Lolth vanished and was believed dead, as was Hestia.
- The same day, the Destroyer rampaged and was finally killed by the united survivors; **[Tiamat](../people/tiamat.md)** claimed his stronghold.
- Aftermath: the shamed drow migrated to what became [Saharun](../places/saharun.md) and into the great woods. The cult resurfaced periodically and was struck down each time — until, at the end of the Fifth Age, it bought a failed slave uprising's survivors cheap and vanished with them into the dead lands (see [Sunhollow Valley](../places/sunhollow-valley.md)).

## Hestia and the Divine Fragments

[Hestia](../people/hestia.md), goddess of hearth and refuge, was killed in this age — deliberately, because [Lolth](../people/lolth.md) understood that a goddess tethered to every welcoming hearth cannot truly die, and made her the engine of her own ascension plan. Hestia's surviving fragments were captured and hidden by Lolth's priestesses near Lolth-linked temple sites; her hearth-marks (see [Hestia's Hearth Pendant](../items/hestias-hearth-pendant.md) and [Bartholomew's Hearth Medallion](../items/bartholomews-hearth-medallion.md)) are manifested by her own power and denote those who greatly assisted her. Her reappearance in [Session 6](../sessions/session-6.md) is the age's history breaking into the present.

## Living Sources

- [Bartholomew Hildebrant](../people/bartholomew-hildebrant.md) — mid-Fourth-Age dwarf (~600 years into the age), Warden of the Veil and Slayer Team member, in stasis ~3,800 years.
- [Rurik Valdren](../people/rurik-valdren.md) — Fourth-Age colonel of the Dragon Watch, alive continuously since.
- [Dead Lands Ghost Druid](../people/dead-lands-ghost-druid.md) — life-bound witness of the valley's fall; guided the assault teams.
- The [Ssar'Velyn Ghost Priestess](../people/ssar-velyn-ghost-priestess.md) and [Temple Guardian](../people/ssar-velyn-temple-guardian.md) — the cult's own surviving authorities.

- The proper operational name of the **coordinated strikes** and of Bartholomew's **Slayer Team** (GM still owes both).

- The full list of divinities killed during Lolth's rise and the coordinated strikes.
- **Fourth-Age dwarven society** beyond the military: the thousand-mile mountain realm, its cities (the [Khaz-Tharol](../places/khaz-tharol-garrison.md) ruins on Driftvale are dated at least this old), and the sanctuary-city obligations ([Sanctuary City](sanctuary-city.md)).
