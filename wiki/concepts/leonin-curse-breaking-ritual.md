# Leonin Curse-Breaking Ritual

The leonin curse-breaking ritual is the unnamed [Ashmane](../factions/ashmane.md) plan revealed in [Session 11](../sessions/session-11.md) to free every people bound by Sunhollow's inherited drow blood curse.

## Name

“Leonin curse-breaking ritual” is a descriptive wiki label. The table did not give the operation a formal name.

## Plan

A secret group of twelve Ashmane intends to seize [Hollowmere](../places/hollowmere.md)'s surviving valley defense matrix and use its interface to hijack the drow ritual. The active demon must first be killed or its corruption purified. If the system can be taken, the leonin believe they can sever the blood curse for every bound lineage rather than only for one clan.

The plan is kept from the full [Sunhollow Council](../factions/sunhollow-council.md) because the Ashmane elder believes disclosure would cause immediate civil war. Rurik is told the material cost. Amir and the Justicar know about the expected Hadozee compulsion and the broad ritual plan but are not told that cost.

## Interruption Risk

The danger follows the point at which the ritual is interrupted:

- An early interruption can kill everyone in the valley.
- A later interruption can kill roughly half.
- A still later interruption can fall chiefly on the leonin who began the ritual.

The record does not establish a guaranteed death toll for an uninterrupted success.

## Material Cost

The ritual will consume every [ether crystal](../items/ether-crystals.md) connected to the valley. The party considers sheltering reserve crystals inside its [Bag of Holding](../items/squad-bag-of-holding.md), but protection is only judged plausible, not confirmed. The exact moment at which active weapons and devices fail is unresolved.

## Dormant Wyvern

The Ashmane elder also warns that the drow purchaser who enslaved the leonin arrived on a great wyvern. Records say it was tended for half a generation before its keepers stopped returning. It has not been seen in a thousand years, and the elder assumes it remains somewhere in the valley in stasis. Its connection to the defense matrix's shutdown is not yet known.

## Session 12 Status

In [Session 12](../sessions/session-12.md), the coalition defeats both demons beneath the [Dead Lands](../places/dead-lands.md), fulfilling the plan's prerequisite of defeating the active demonic threat. The Church begins preparing and carrying out purification at the complex, but the leonin ritual has not been shown taking control of the defense matrix or breaking the blood curse by the recording's end.

[Rurik](../people/rurik-valdren.md) warns that the harengon evacuation is unlikely to finish before the ritual consumes the connected crystals. The party decides to gather crystals while resting before returning to the valley's people. Neither that decision nor the continued operation of their equipment establishes that crystals stored in a Bag of Holding will survive the eventual ritual.
