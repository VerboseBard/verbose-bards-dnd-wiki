# Ashmane

Ashmane is the dominant cat clan of [Sunhollow Valley](../places/sunhollow-valley.md).

## Identity

Ashmane's prestige comes from memory as much as power. In the history disclosed in [Session 11](../sessions/session-11.md), humans enslaved the leonin on an island roughly 1,400 years ago. Four centuries later, an escaped ancestor returned and led an uprising that nearly succeeded, but promised allies never arrived. The survivors were sold to a drow purchaser, and the inherited blood curse descends from that sale.

That history is not a story of the drow preserving the survivors. It is a second enslavement following a failed liberation. Ashmane still carries the legacy of having struck first and the grievance that earlier heroes promised aid but did not come.

That memory gives the clan seniority in the eyes of many valley families.

## Members

Ashmane includes:

- Leonin / lionfolk
- Tabaxi / catfolk
- Eluran
- local lynx-like cat lines

## Current Leadership

The seated elder of Ashmane is [Siv Halven](../people/siv-halven.md), a leonine old-guard leader who favors isolation, discipline, and strict stewardship.

Its primary valley stronghold is [Ashmane Hold](../places/ashmane-hold.md).

Known race speakers include:

- [Siv Halven](../people/siv-halven.md) for the Leonin
- Runa Keld for the Tabaxi
- Leora Svan for the Eluran

## Politics

Ashmane is strongly associated with [the Old Charge](old-charge.md). It treats outside contact as a danger first and an opportunity second, and it resists any easy restoration of the lost rabbit seat.

In [Session 10](../sessions/session-10.md), Ashmane is the most reluctant clan to accept [Rurik Valdren](../people/rurik-valdren.md)'s mining agreement. In [Session 11](../sessions/session-11.md), a secret group of twelve leonin reveals the [Leonin Curse-Breaking Ritual](../concepts/leonin-curse-breaking-ritual.md). They keep it from the seated council because they believe open disclosure would trigger civil war.
