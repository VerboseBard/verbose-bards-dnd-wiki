# The Sentinels of the Veil

The Sentinels of the Veil were the professional order of Fourth-Age dwarven warriors who manned [the Dragon Watch](../places/dragon-watch.md) and guarded the Veil — holding the line against the undead wastes as the dwarves' penance for summoning the first dragon in the Second Age.

An individual member is a **Sentinel of the Veil**. Every dwarf served: out of training, a soldier went to the wall for a six-month rotation, and the order's veterans commanded it in shifts measured in decades. Its forces held constant, around-the-clock war against undead hordes, demons, wasteland creatures, and the ancient dragon threat above.

## Structure

- **The Sentinels of the Veil** — the wall-defending order itself.
- **The Demon Hunters** — elite troops selected from the regular military and wall defenders for specialized training against demons, devils, powerful undead, summoned creatures, and similar supernatural threats. [Bartholomew Hildebrant](../people/bartholomew-hildebrant.md)'s arcane banishment tattoo marked him as one of these.
- **The Slayer Teams** — the elite special-forces formations in which Demon Hunters served. Members of the Slayer Teams bore the title **Warden**: a formal office in the family of titles like Justicar, Templar, or Adjudicator, not merely a job description. A Warden's position was that of a **champion of his goddess** — which is why Bartholomew, Warden of the Veil, woke from stasis wearing [a hearth medallion](../items/bartholomews-hearth-medallion.md) of [Hestia](../people/hestia.md).

A separate strike-team branch specialized in the undead proper; the Slayer Teams handled demons and devils.

## Known Members

- **Colonel [Rurik Valdren](../people/rurik-valdren.md)** — commanded a section of the Dragon Watch for some two hundred years; was on the wall when the Breaking came.
- **Captain [Bartholomew Hildebrant](../people/bartholomew-hildebrant.md)** — Warden of the Veil, Demon Hunter of the Slayer Teams; served under Rurik ~3,800 years ago before his reassignment against the cult of [Lolth](../people/lolth.md) and the assault on [Ssar'Velyn Temple](../places/ssar-velyn-temple.md).

## Naming Note

The order's name was locked in during later worldbuilding discussion rather than spoken in the original transcripts; an earlier draft term, "the Vindicators," is superseded by **Sentinels of the Veil**. The proper name of Bartholomew's individual Slayer Team was lost from the notes and is still owed by the record.
