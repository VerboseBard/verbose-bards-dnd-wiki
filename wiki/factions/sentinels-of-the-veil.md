# The Sentinels of the Veil

The Sentinels of the Veil were the professional dwarven order that manned [the Dragon Watch](../places/dragon-watch.md) and guarded the Veil. Their defensive tradition began in the [Third Age](../concepts/third-age.md), after the failed attempt to kill the First Dragon created the Dead Lands, and endured as the dwarves' penance through the Fourth Age and beyond.

An individual member is a **Sentinel of the Veil**. Every dwarf served: out of training, a soldier went to the wall for a six-month rotation, and the order's veterans commanded it in shifts measured in decades. Its forces held constant, around-the-clock war against undead hordes, demons, wasteland creatures, and the ancient dragon threat above.

## Structure

- **The Sentinels of the Veil** — the wall-defending order itself.
- **The Demon Hunters** — elite troops selected from the regular military and wall defenders for specialized training against demons, devils, powerful undead, summoned creatures, and similar supernatural threats. [Bartholomew Hildebrant](../people/bartholomew-hildebrant.md)'s arcane banishment tattoo marked him as one of these.
- **The Slayer Teams** — the elite special-forces formations in which Demon Hunters served. Members of the Slayer Teams bore the title **Warden**: a formal office in the family of titles like Justicar, Templar, or Adjudicator, not merely a job description. A Warden's position was that of a **champion of his goddess**, which is why Bartholomew woke from stasis wearing [a hearth medallion](../items/bartholomews-hearth-medallion.md) of [Hestia](../people/hestia.md).

A separate strike-team branch specialized in the undead proper; the Slayer Teams handled demons and devils.

## Known Members

- **Colonel [Rurik Valdren](../people/rurik-valdren.md)** — commanded a section of the Dragon Watch for some two hundred years; was on the wall when the Breaking came.
- **Captain [Bartholomew Hildebrant](../people/bartholomew-hildebrant.md)** — Warden of the Veil and Champion of Hestia; served under Rurik roughly 3,800 years ago before his reassignment against the cult of [Lolth](../people/lolth.md) and the assault on [Ssar'Velyn Temple](../places/ssar-velyn-temple.md).

## Naming Note

The order's name was locked in during later worldbuilding discussion rather than spoken in the original transcripts; an earlier draft term, "the Vindicators," is superseded by **Sentinels of the Veil**. The proper name of Bartholomew's individual Slayer Team was lost from the notes and is still owed by the record.

## Session 10 Confirmation

[Session 10](../sessions/session-10.md) states the hierarchy on-stream: Dragon Watch posting, Sentinel of the Veil, selection and specialized Demon Hunter training, assignment to a Slayer Team, and the member title **Warden**. The modern Justicars were modeled in part on these old units, although [Rurik Valdren](../people/rurik-valdren.md) considers the imitation incomplete.

## Bartholomew's Earlier Service and Reassignment

[Session 12](../sessions/session-12.md) recovers a stage of [Bartholomew Hildebrant](../people/bartholomew-hildebrant.md)'s service before his mixed-race assignment. He was already serving in dwarven special forces that hunted and killed demons. His unit raided a blood-consuming cult whose dead included firstborn children of powerful families.

Months later, his order's head praised the raid but explained that influential families might have backed the cultists; proof was still lacking. He also warned of growing support for refined demonic power and a small but growing military faction seeking an army combining demonic and undead power. The political pressure prompted the team's reassignment to a special branch working with elves, halflings, and other peoples, where they could continue hunting demons while the controversy subsided.

Sources: [the remembered raid and reassignment](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eqYr6r-jOVk&t=15166), 4:12:46–4:22:32; GM confirmation of the service sequence, September 6, 2026.
