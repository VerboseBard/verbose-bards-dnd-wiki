# Verbose Bard's D&D Wikipedia

## About This Wikipedia

This player-safe Wikipedia is built from actual-play transcripts, adjudicated canon, and setting material already cleared for publication. It keeps the material cross-linked so people, places, factions, concepts, items, campaigns, and sessions can be followed like an encyclopedia.

## Current Age

The current campaign begins in [Year 11](concepts/timeline.md) of the [Seventh Age](concepts/seventh-age.md), eleven years after [the Concurrence](concepts/the-concurrence.md). By [Session 13](sessions/session-13.md), the party has survived the [Bloody Fifth](factions/bloody-fifth.md), served in [Ash's Hounds](factions/ashs-hounds.md), helped enable [Kreen](factions/kreen.md) surrender through [control collars](items/kreen-control-collars.md), become military contractors, claimed the hidden [Kex](places/kex.md), and rediscovered [Sunhollow Valley](places/sunhollow-valley.md) beneath a crisis rooted in [Lolth's Fourth-Age rise](concepts/lolths-rise-and-the-great-crusade.md).

In Session 13, the [leonin curse-breaking ritual](concepts/leonin-curse-breaking-ritual.md) succeeds and all [Driftvale](places/driftvale.md) fog disperses. The victory costs the party [Lord Inquisitor Amir Voss](people/amir-voss.md), destroys [Mistress Selyra Vex'ryn](people/mistress-selyra-vexryn.md)'s escape route and then Selyra herself, and leaves her archer brother alive. The [Justicar](people/the-justicar.md) and Bartholomew are at the temple while the rest of the party remains at Hollowmere; the temple purge and political aftermath have not yet been played.

For a compact setting overview, see the [Seventh Age Setting Bible](concepts/seventh-age-setting-bible.md).
