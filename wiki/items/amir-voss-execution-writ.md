# Amir Voss's Execution Writ

Amir Voss's execution writ is a conditional Church warrant signed by [Lord Inquisitor Amir Voss](../people/amir-voss.md) and entrusted by [the Justicar](../people/the-justicar.md) to [Bartholomew Hildebrant](../people/bartholomew-hildebrant.md).

## Condition

In [Session 10](../sessions/session-10.md), the Justicar says Bartholomew should use the writ only if Amir crosses from greed into evil. The document is therefore a safeguard against a future abuse of authority, not proof that Amir has already fallen.

## Institutional Meaning

In [Session 11](../sessions/session-11.md), Amir confirms under the [Truth Lantern](truth-lantern.md) that the signature is his. He explains that watchers within the Church accept conditional warrants against themselves: Inquisitors investigate corruption inside righteous institutions, while Justicars hold the Inquisition's writs.

The warrant does not make the system automatic. Its use still depends on the holder's judgment about when the condition has been met.

## Session 13 Recovery

In [Session 13](../sessions/session-13.md), Bartholomew discovers the missing writ in the hand of an enemy paladin. He uses the encounter's specific reaction ruling to seize it, briefly loses it again, and recovers and secures it before it can activate. The session does not establish how the document left his custody, who arranged the transfer, or whether Amir ever met its stated moral condition.

Amir is later killed by the archer's poison and disintegration sequence, a separate event that occurs after the writ is safe. The writ does not execute Amir and does not deliver a final moral judgment on him.
