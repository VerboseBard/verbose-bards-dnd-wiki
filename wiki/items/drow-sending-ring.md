# Drow Sending Ring

The drow sending ring is a communication ring given to the party by an unnamed female drow on the temple-side return route in [Session 12](../sessions/session-12.md). Its sending stone is built into the ring, and later identification reveals that it also transmits a periodic location beacon.

## The Drow Contact

The woman joins [Rurik Valdren](../people/rurik-valdren.md) and the departing party by stepping through darkness. She discusses their former companion and the return of their shared belongings, then leaves the ring as a means of future contact. Her proper name is not established in this exchange.

The ring's contact function is openly offered. Its periodic location ping becomes known during the party's later examination. The record does not establish the beacon's range, interval, recipient or exact accuracy.

This ring is separate from Rurik's dove-decorated ring used to shield their earlier conversation.
