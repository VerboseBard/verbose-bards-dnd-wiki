# Living Storage Bag

The living storage bag is the second bag recovered with the party's shared property on the temple-side route in [Session 12](../sessions/session-12.md). It has the appearance of a bag and contains a dimensional storage space. This is a descriptive page title; the session also calls it a “Bag of Devour.”

## Recovery and Discovery

The party seeks the property left with [Bevar Lurton](../people/bevar-lurton.md) after the temple split. Following a conversation with an unnamed drow woman, the returning group finds the bag beside [Rurik Valdren](../people/rurik-valdren.md)'s portal. Members can still reach inside and withdraw their money, but [Jose](../people/jose.md) notices something has changed.

Later examination reveals that the bag itself is alive. It is entered as a second bag in the party inventory and remains distinct from the original [military-issued squad Bag of Holding](squad-bag-of-holding.md). Who changed or replaced it, and when, are not established.

## Known Limits

Its storage function works during the recovery. Its full nature, feeding needs, dangers and other abilities still require investigation. No attack or destruction of the party's stored property is shown in this session. Its ability to protect [ether crystals](ether-crystals.md) from the planned valley ritual has not been tested.
