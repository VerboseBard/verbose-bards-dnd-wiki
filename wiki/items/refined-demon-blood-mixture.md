# Refined Demon-Blood Mixture

The refined demon-blood mixture is a dangerous purple liquid found in the second chamber of the [demon-draining complex](../concepts/demon-draining-engine.md) in [Session 12](../sessions/session-12.md). [Jefferson Stone](../people/sergeant-jefferson-stone.md) collects a bottle after the battle.

## Description and Collection

The liquid has a consistency likened to the difference between raw and refined [ether crystals](ether-crystals.md). Through [Hestia's Hearth Pendant](hestias-hearth-pendant.md), Jefferson feels the familiar hearth presence. It describes the substance as refined demon blood mixed with divine energy and crystals, and warns that it is extremely dangerous. That composition is the presence's explanation.

Jefferson drinks a healing potion and uses the empty bottle to take a sample. Brief contact strips the flesh from part of his hand before it grows back. Some of his nanites are destroyed, but the remaining nanites are enhanced; he chooses a one-point increase to Strength. The resolved interaction causes no hit-point loss.

## Distinction and Uncertainty

This sample comes from the demon complex in Session 12. It is separate from the [Demonic Ichor Vial](demonic-ichor-vial.md) Jefferson collected in the temple in Session 9. Its identity with the older [Demonic Dwarven Goo](../concepts/demonic-dwarven-goo.md) is not established.

The mixture's wider uses and long-term effects remain unknown. Jefferson's particular interaction does not establish that the substance safely enhances everyone who touches it.
