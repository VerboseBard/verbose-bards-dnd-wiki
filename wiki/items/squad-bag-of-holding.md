# Squad Bag of Holding

The squad's Bag of Holding is a military-issued extradimensional storage item carried by the After the War party.

## Campaign Use

The party receives the bag when [Ash's Hounds](../factions/ashs-hounds.md) are dissolved in [Session 3](../sessions/session-3.md). By [Session 4](../sessions/session-4.md), it keeps much of the party's wealth outside the searched barracks lockers.

In [Session 11](../sessions/session-11.md), the party considers whether the bag's extradimensional interior can protect stored [ether crystals](ether-crystals.md) from the leonin curse-breaking ritual. The GM judges the attempt plausible and worth making, but survival of the crystals is not confirmed. The exact number stored in the bag is also unclear in the transcript.
