# Squad Bag of Holding

The squad's Bag of Holding is a military-issued extradimensional storage item carried by the After the War party.

## Campaign Use

The party receives the bag when [Ash's Hounds](../factions/ashs-hounds.md) are dissolved in [Session 3](../sessions/session-3.md). By [Session 4](../sessions/session-4.md), it keeps much of the party's wealth outside the searched barracks lockers.

In [Session 11](../sessions/session-11.md), the party considers whether the bag's extradimensional interior can protect stored [ether crystals](ether-crystals.md) from the leonin curse-breaking ritual. The GM judges the attempt plausible and worth making, but survival of the crystals is not confirmed. The exact number stored in the bag is also unclear in the transcript.

## Session 12: Two Storage Bags

In [Session 12](../sessions/session-12.md), the party recovers a second storage bag with shared property left on [Bevar Lurton](../people/bevar-lurton.md)'s side of the temple split. That returned bag is later revealed to be a [living storage creature](living-storage-bag.md). The revelation concerns the recovered second bag; it does not establish that the original military-issued squad bag is alive.

The returning party plans to gather ether crystals into its available storage before heading back toward town. The recording ends before the valley-wide ritual, so the bags' ability to preserve crystals through that working remains untested. See the [Session 12 recording, 4:34:34–4:39:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eqYr6r-jOVk&t=16474) and [4:50:53–4:58:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eqYr6r-jOVk&t=17453).
