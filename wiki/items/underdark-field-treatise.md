# Underdark Field Treatise

The Underdark field treatise is a rough, homemade leather-bound book recovered by [Jose](../people/jose.md) from the demon complex after the battle in [Session 12](../sessions/session-12.md). Its recorded subject is the monsters and flora of the [Underdark, now called the Upside Down](../places/underdark-upside-down.md). The page title is descriptive; no formal book title is established.

## Recovery

Jose searches for material relevant to his monster-hunter background before Church personnel confiscate the complex's records. The book catches his attention because of its rough construction, and he recognizes its practical subject matter.

Several pages have been deliberately torn out. The removed material, its importance, and who removed it remain unknown.

The party also retrieves three ornate elven books with silver inlay, and Rurik pockets a smaller book concealed within another volume. These are distinct finds; the collector of the ornate books is not securely assigned in the record. Bartholomew's [dwarven survivor's journal](dwarven-survivors-journal.md) is another separate book.
