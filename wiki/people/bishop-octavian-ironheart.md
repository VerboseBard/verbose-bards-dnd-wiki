# Cardinal Octavian Ironheart

Octavian Ironheart is an orc priest of [Gorthak](gorthak.md), the war god of [Norvindr](../places/norvindr.md)'s [Gobfolk Alliance](../factions/gobfolk-alliance.md). He was a bishop at [Ice Watch](../places/ice-watch.md) when the [Moon Stone Collectors](../factions/moon-stone-collectors.md) dealt with him. He is now the Cardinal of the War God.

## Bishop at Ice Watch

Ironheart is the [Norvindr](../places/norvindr.md) church authority who hears the [Moon Stone Collectors](../factions/moon-stone-collectors.md)' report after the missing wagon and abducted travelers incident.

He confirms that the wagon reached the city empty, arranges [Citadel](../places/radiant-citadel.md) access for the party, and warns that the correct portal expert is not immediately available.

Ironheart is practical rather than theatrical. He gives the party a travel token, keeps the process moving, and tries to keep the church from losing face as the [Concurrence](../concepts/the-concurrence.md) closes in.

## Cardinal of the War God

Ironheart's church later merged into what became the [Conclave of Churches](../factions/conclave-of-churches.md).

As an orc, he comes from a people used to being on the outs with many of the other gods. That made him a compromise: a religious leader who was also a major figure from a warlike people, with a war god. That is why he ended up in charge during the [Kreen War](../concepts/kreen-war.md).
