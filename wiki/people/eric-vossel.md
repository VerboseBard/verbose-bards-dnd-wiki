# Eric Vossel

Eric Vossel is [Rurik Valdren](rurik-valdren.md)'s right hand — a sorlock (sorcerer-warlock) who serves as Rurik's chief caster and aide.

## Role

The party first encountered him as the greeter and usher at [Calvin's Curios](../places/calvins-curios.md) auctions in [Sin](../places/sin.md). In [Session 9](../sessions/session-9.md) he teleports into [Sunhollow Valley](../places/sunhollow-valley.md) at Rurik's side, keeps the party hasted, and casts repeated *greater restoration* to clear exhaustion during the forced march back to [Hollowmere](../places/hollowmere.md) — burning expensive [mana potions](../items/mana-potions.md) to sustain the casting ("officers use them; they're just addictive").

## Name Note

Caption drift briefly conflated him with the Inquisition contact ("Air Voss / Eric Voss"). They are distinct people: the Inquisition contact is [Lord Inquisitor Amir Voss](amir-voss.md); Eric Vossel is Rurik's man. Whether Vossel relates to the Voss family names in the record is unknown.

## Session 13

Eric meets the travelling party at Hollowmere and offers a choice: remain in the defense or accelerate the refugee portal operation by an estimated two to three times. The party sends him to the evacuation work. This is a forecast of improved throughput, not a final passenger count or proof that the evacuation completed.

The Session 13 table briefly uses Talin/Duran-like wording for this person. The GM later confirms it was a name mix-up: Eric is Rurik's aide in this scene, and [Talin Duren](talin-duren.md) remains a separate Longbough figure.
