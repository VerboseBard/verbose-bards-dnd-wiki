# The First Dragon (the Destroyer)

The First Dragon — called **the Destroyer** in [Rurik Valdren](rurik-valdren.md)'s telling — is the dragon the dwarves pulled into the world in the [Second Age](../concepts/second-age.md), in retaliation for the Fey transforming the giants into dwarves. Per Rurik: *"he's the dragon that defined the second, the third, and the fourth ages, and set up all the events of the fifth."*

## Disambiguation

Several beings carry "Destroyer" titles in the record. This page is the **Second-Age summoned dragon** only — distinct from [Lucius](lucius.md) (the Great Deceiver, "Destroyer of Worlds," ended at the close of the [Sixth Age](../concepts/sixth-age.md)) and from the [Opaal Old One](../concepts/opaal-old-one.md) (also titled Destroyer of Worlds in Opaal prophecy).

## History

- **Second Age:** Pulled into the material world by the dwarves as the war with the Fey turned against them. Its arrival was the first known breach in reality. The dragon ended the war by killing almost everyone on both sides and eventually settled on a mountain. The Fey withdrawal into their own realms at the end of the age became the second known breach.
- **Third Age:** The dwarves attempted to kill the dragon as restitution for the calamity they had caused. They failed. Its answering rampage lasted nearly fifty years, killed millions, and created the vast [Dead Lands](../places/dead-lands.md). After it returned to its mountain, the dwarves began their penance-war against the undead and built the defenses that became [the Dragon Watch](../places/dragon-watch.md).
- **The Dragon Law:** An unknown Third-Age rewriter used the death energy of the catastrophe to bind dragon lives to reality's defenses, restrict the number of living dragons, and place successor eggs into conditional stasis. See [Dragon Guardians and Succession](../concepts/dragon-guardians.md).
- **Fourth Age:** [Lolth](lolth.md) went to the dragon and made an alliance — *"he helped her kill a god and steal its power"* — the first mortal deicide, the foundation of her seizure of the elven holy valley and the corruption of [Ssar'Velyn Temple](../places/ssar-velyn-temple.md). The [Dead Lands Ghost Druid](dead-lands-ghost-druid.md)'s account and the temple mosaics also place a betrayer with foresight, [the Weaver](../concepts/the-weaver.md), behind the arrangement.
- **The End:** On the same day the coordinated Fourth-Age strikes hit Lolth's power centers, the dragon went on another rampage. The surviving forces of the age pulled together and **killed the Destroyer once and for all**.
- **Succession:** With his destruction, one of his two principal children made herself known: **[Tiamat](tiamat.md)**, Mother of Dragons, who took his stronghold in the mountains. Her brother became the protector of dragons. Tiamat's own later history is told on her page and in the [Hunt for Black Vipers](../campaign/hunt-for-black-vipers.md) material.
