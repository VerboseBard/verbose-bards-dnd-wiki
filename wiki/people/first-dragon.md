# The First Dragon (the Destroyer)

The First Dragon — called **the Destroyer** in [Rurik Valdren](rurik-valdren.md)'s telling — is the dragon the dwarves summoned into the world in the [Second Age](../concepts/second-age.md), in retaliation for the fae cursing the giants into dwarves. Per Rurik: *"he's the dragon that defined the second, the third, and the fourth ages, and set up all the events of the fifth."*

## Disambiguation

Several beings carry "Destroyer" titles in the record. This page is the **Second-Age summoned dragon** only — distinct from [Lucius](lucius.md) (the Great Deceiver, "Destroyer of Worlds," ended at the close of the [Sixth Age](../concepts/sixth-age.md)) and from the [Opaal Old One](../concepts/opaal-old-one.md) (also titled Destroyer of Worlds in Opaal prophecy).

## History

- **Second Age:** Summoned by the dwarves as the war with the fae turned against them. The dragon ended the war by killing almost everyone on both sides. It could not be killed, and it settled on a mountain.
- **Second–Third Ages:** It stayed on its mount for two ages. The devastation it left — and the undead-scoured wastes — became the dwarves' burden: their penance was [the Dragon Watch](../places/dragon-watch.md), holding the wall against the wastes for thousands of years.
- **Fourth Age:** [Lolth](lolth.md) went to the dragon and made an alliance — *"he helped her kill a god and steal its power"* — the first mortal deicide, the foundation of her seizure of the elven holy valley and the corruption of [Ssar'Velyn Temple](../places/ssar-velyn-temple.md). (The [Dead Lands Ghost Druid](dead-lands-ghost-druid.md)'s account; the temple mosaics add ⚠that a betrayer with *foresight* — [the Weaver](../concepts/the-weaver.md) — stood behind the arrangement.)
- **The End:** On the same day the coordinated Fourth-Age strikes hit Lolth's power centers, the dragon went on a rampage. The surviving forces of the age pulled together and **killed the Destroyer once and for all**.
- **Succession:** With his destruction, his mate made herself known: **[Tiamat](tiamat.md)**, mother of dragons, who took his stronghold in the mountains. Her own later history (legend rather than god; bound in the Hells; freed and ultimately destroyed) is told on her page and in the [Hunt for Black Vipers](../campaign/hunt-for-black-vipers.md) material.
