# Gorthak

Gorthak is the war god of [Norvindr](../places/norvindr.md)'s [Gobfolk Alliance](../factions/gobfolk-alliance.md). He is associated with conflict, victory, and self-improvement.

## Faith

Gorthak's faith centres on discipline. Under Norvindr's old ways, people stayed in line and followed the strictures of the god of war: the war with yourself. On the ice, anyone who did not work hard and keep to that discipline did not survive, and neither did their family.

After the [Concurrence](../concepts/the-concurrence.md), Norvindr's north thawed, and many of the young broke with the old rules and went north. The south stayed frozen. The monsters that needed the cold moved there, so the old ways had to be held to even more strictly.

## Temple

The [Temple of Gorthak's Triumph](../places/temple-of-gorthaks-triumph.md) stands at [Ice Watch](../places/ice-watch.md), the seat of religious power on [Norvindr](../places/norvindr.md).

## Clergy and Followers

- [Cardinal Octavian Ironheart](bishop-octavian-ironheart.md), the orc Cardinal of the War God, formerly a bishop at [Ice Watch](../places/ice-watch.md).
- [Luka](luka.md) of the [Moon Stone Collectors](../factions/moon-stone-collectors.md), who is devoted to Gorthak as a god of strength, struggle, and trials.
