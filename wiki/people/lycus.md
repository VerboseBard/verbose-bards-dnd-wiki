# Lycus

Lycus is Zach's dragonborn survivor from the Bloody Fifth and, by Session 10, captain of the mercenary unit attached to the Inquisition expedition entering [Sunhollow Valley](../places/sunhollow-valley.md).

## Bloody Fifth

Lycus began as a corporal in Squad 15 during Session 1, carrying a shotgun and surviving after being downed and stabilized. He is one of the veteran survivors whose accelerated dragonborn growth makes only a few years of elapsed life feel much longer.

## Session 10

Lycus leads the expedition's forward mercenary element and greets the party warmly. [Rose](rose.md), another Bloody Fifth survivor, serves as his lieutenant.

His argument with [Sgt. Jefferson Stone](sergeant-jefferson-stone.md) defines his current outlook. He believes a paid soldier must report the terrain and dangers honestly, but he refuses to treat civilians as enemies. His line is simple: "We don't hurt civilians. We kill the enemy."

Lycus also describes the dragonborn as a wartime people bred from dragons and yuan-ti to fight the Kreen. He will not return to [Saharun](../places/saharun.md), where the dragonborn experiment and the plague that sterilized their dragon parents remain sources of fear and rejection.

## Session 11

Lycus leads the mercenary element during the march from [Windcrest Roost](../places/windcrest-roost.md) to the demon complex. Mercenaries participate in the barrage that kills the Hadozee contingent, but the transcript does not identify Lycus personally as one of the shooters. The event places his Session 10 distinction between civilians and enemies under immediate pressure.
