# Rose (Bloody Fifth)

Rose is Nate's vulpin Bloody Fifth survivor and, by Session 10, lieutenant to [Lycus](lycus.md) in the mercenary unit attached to the Inquisition expedition. She is distinct from [Rose Qinlee](rose-qinlee.md).

## Bloody Fifth

Rose served as an enlisted Squad 14 rifle user and backup squad leader during Session 1. She survived severe wounds and was evacuated rather than dying with the unit.

## Session 10

Rose accompanies Lycus in the expedition's vanguard and reunites warmly with the party. She shares Lycus's boundary that civilians are not legitimate targets.

During the march to [Windcrest Roost](../places/windcrest-roost.md), Rose trades chocolate for the valley's enormous strawberries and discusses the scattered vulpin population. She knows of a few families on [Norvindr](../places/norvindr.md), three packs on [Unity](../places/unity.md), and a family connected to the Citadel school who were killed after its defenses failed.

## Session 11

Rose joins the expedition into the [Dead Lands](../places/dead-lands.md). After the Hadozee contingent is killed before it has attacked, she observes that they had not become traitors yet. The line preserves her Session 10 refusal to erase the distinction between civilians and enemies, even while her mercenary unit is part of the force that carries out the barrage.
