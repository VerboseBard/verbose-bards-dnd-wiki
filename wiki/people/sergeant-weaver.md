# Sergeant Weaver

Sergeant Weaver is a fat, old goblin sergeant of the [Fort Victory](../places/fort-victory.md) garrison, first met in [Session 3](../sessions/session-3.md) smoking a long pipe near the supply convoy and directing the party toward the officers' quarters to roust [Lieutenant Garrick Hume](lieutenant-garrick-hume.md).

## Description

An old-timer of the garrison's slovenly era — comfortable, unhurried, adjacent to the fort's corrupt normal without being one of its named ring figures. His name is attested identically on both independent transcripts of Session 3.

## Name Disambiguation

Per GM ruling (2026-07-31): the name is coincidence. Sergeant Weaver has **no connection** to [the Weaver](../concepts/the-weaver.md) — the powerful entity of the Second and Fourth Ages that the crashed ship's insane security AI believed was manipulating everything (and whose fear drove that AI to kill the [Kex](../places/kex.md)'s crew). He is also unrelated to the [Goblin Quartermaster Assistant](goblin-quartermaster-assistant.md).
