# The Silk Parlor Companions

The Silk Parlor Companions are the elite courtesan-intelligencers of [The Silk Parlor](../places/silk-parlor.md) in [Sin](../places/sin.md), serving [Mistress Selyra Vex'ryn](mistress-selyra-vexryn.md). They are far more than hostesses: magical courtesans, mental healers, listeners, and the eyes of the [Silk Parlor Network](../factions/silk-parlor-network.md). During the party's Session 3 dinner they had already read the party's classified files, and the entire meal was a soft interrogation about whether the party would report Selyra's scavengers in the fog.

In [Session 9](../sessions/session-9.md) it is learned that Selyra and all of her women vanished from Sin just ahead of the [Inquisition](../factions/inquisition.md)'s raid on the Parlor.

## The Eight

1. **Velrith Nyx** — drow; the chained one with the goblet. Presence: dominant, unshakable, enforcer energy. Likely one of Selyra's most trusted. (Sat with Jose at the Session 3 dinner; the GM spelled the name V-E-L-R-I-T-H N-Y-X on stream.)
2. **Kaelis Varn** — wood elf; dark-haired, observant, holding her drink carefully. Presence: watcher, listener, information gatherer — reads people more than she speaks. (Greeted the party at the door and worked [Sgt. Jefferson Stone](sergeant-jefferson-stone.md) through dinner; spelled K-A-E-L-I-S V-A-R-N.)
3. **Serenya Vael** — pale, centered, seated prominently. Presence: calm, refined, almost royal. Likely handles high-end clients.
4. **Draevira Sol** — standing behind, composed and poised. Presence: silent authority. Could be part of internal security or oversight.
5. **Lethira Morn** — blonde, elegant, almost noble in bearing. Presence: approachable but elevated. Bridges common guests to elite spaces.
6. **Myrren Dusk** — seated, relaxed but attentive. Presence: conversational, emotionally intelligent. Extracts secrets through comfort.
7. **Zyraen Vex** — winter-aspect eladrin; standing right, sharp gaze, composed. Presence: cold, precise, intimidating. Likely handles problem guests. (Delighted in tormenting the woman-phobic [Bevar Lurton](bevar-lurton.md) at dinner; spelled Z-Y-R-A-E-N. Note the **Vex** surname shared with Selyra Vex'ryn — possible house name; unconfirmed.)
8. **Thalira Noct** — far right, composed and reserved. Presence: quiet danger. The kind that speaks once — and ends conversations.

One of the companions once slipped and referred to Selyra as "mother" — as a title (Matron Mother) — during the Session 3 dinner.
