# The Justicar

The Justicar is the nameless Church champion accompanying [Lord Inquisitor Amir Voss](amir-voss.md) into [Sunhollow Valley](../places/sunhollow-valley.md). He surrendered his personal name in service and is known only by his office.

## Nature and Authority

The Justicar is an aasimar. This identification, previously near-certain from the Session 10 witnesses, was confirmed by GM ruling on 2026-08-16. He is also winged; whether the wings are permanent or manifest only under divine exertion remains unresolved. In armor he has an overwhelming presence; above the table, the GM describes a thirty-foot aura granting a +10 bonus to Charisma checks.

Justicars carry fragments of their gods' power. They can bypass the [Divine Gate](../concepts/divine-gate.md) and, at the cost of burning themselves out, briefly host a divine avatar. The office is one of the smaller [Conclave of Churches](../factions/conclave-of-churches.md) orders, with roughly 800 experienced Justicars and another 10,000 in training after the War of the Gods.

## Session 10

At the valley gate, the Justicar prevents Amir and [Rurik Valdren](rurik-valdren.md)'s feud from controlling the encounter. He accepts the Owlin elder's guest-right, recognizes Rurik's current legal protection, and orders Amir not to cause trouble.

The Justicar later summons [Bartholomew Hildebrant](bartholomew-hildebrant.md) to his warded tent. He studied the Fourth-Age Wardens before joining the Church and tells Bartholomew, "You were my inspiration."

He favors destroying the active demon first, then attempting to break the valley's blood curse rather than treating every bound person as a willing cultist. He shares the Owlin blood-curse diagram, the 800-year breeding program, Church research on [Hestia](hestia.md), [Lolth](lolth.md), [Lucius](lucius.md), and [the Weaver](../concepts/the-weaver.md), and the dwarf-skin journal recording the fall of the dwarven capital.

## The Writ

The Justicar believes Amir's judgment has been clouded by greed, although he also says Amir has served well and believes he is protecting the greater number. He gives Bartholomew a signed writ of execution to use only if Amir crosses the line from greed into evil. The assessment is the Justicar's judgment, not an objective ruling that every accusation against Amir is true.

## Session 11

The Justicar's private safeguards are placed within a larger reciprocal system: senior watchers sign conditional [execution writs](../items/amir-voss-execution-writ.md) against themselves, with Justicars holding the Inquisition's warrants. Amir confirms that the signature on Bartholomew's writ is his own.

At the demon complex, the Justicar identifies two demons and takes the stronger one while Amir and five Inquisitors turn toward the lesser. The Justicar manifests his wings as his power rises. The party follows him partly to prevent him from spending the divine spark that would kill him. The record does not establish whether he knew of or approved Amir's earlier plan to kill the Hadozee contingent.

## Session 12

In [Session 12](../sessions/session-12.md), the Justicar imbues the coalition's weapons with radiant power and fights the greater demon while [Alistair Hooley](alistair-hooley.md) struggles against it through the metaphysical tether. The demon is disintegrated, and the remaining enemies in the chamber are cleared by the end of the fourth round. The Justicar survives without having to burn himself out in the sacrificial release he had prepared as a last resort.

He immediately rushes to the other battlefront. When party members follow him into that chamber, the lesser demon is dead and several Inquisitors are gravely wounded or awaiting revival. He uses his remaining power to bring fallen allies back. The battle leaves no additional permanent losses beyond the Hadozee contingent killed before the assault.

The Justicar remains at the complex for the Church's purification work. Late in the session, he rises from meditation to begin another four or five hours of cleansing and asks [Bartholomew](bartholomew-hildebrant.md) to help watch the entrance. Defeating the demons has made purification possible; neither the cleansing nor the valley's separate [curse-breaking ritual](../concepts/leonin-curse-breaking-ritual.md) is established as complete at the session's end.
