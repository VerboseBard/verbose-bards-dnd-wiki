# The Dragon Watch

The Dragon Watch was the named military station and command on the enormous Fourth-Age dwarven wall that sealed off the undead wastelands. The physical fortification can also be called the Great Wall; Dragon Watch was the posting where [Bartholomew Hildebrant](../people/bartholomew-hildebrant.md) served under Colonel [Rurik Valdren](../people/rurik-valdren.md).

## Description

A wall on the scale of legend, garrisoned and *fighting* twenty-four hours a day: undead hordes out of the wastes, demons that manifested along the line, wasteland monstrosities — and at the heights, the ancient dragon threat itself. Dwarven military culture ran through it: every dwarf out of training served a six-month rotation on the wall before anything else.

## Penance

The dwarves held this line for roughly 4,500 years as penance. Their people summoned the first dragon in the Second Age to end the war with the fae — and the dragon's devastation, and the undead-scoured wastes it left, became the dwarves' burden to contain on behalf of everyone else. Per Rurik, that discipline is also why the dwarves conquered the world in the Fourth and Fifth Ages: an entire civilization forged by the wall.

The order that manned it was [the Sentinels of the Veil](../factions/sentinels-of-the-veil.md).

## Fall

Rurik commanded a section of the wall for two hundred years and was standing on it when the Breaking came. With the world shattered and the defenders scattered, most believed the old Veil regions fell to the undead. What remains of the wall in the Seventh Age — and what still stalks the far side of it — is unknown.
