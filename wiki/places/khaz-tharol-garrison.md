# Khaz-Tharol Garrison

Khaz-Tharol Garrison is the professional military outpost built into the ancient dwarven **Khaz-Tharol Ruins** in [Driftvale](driftvale.md)'s mountainous gate region. It is the site where the [Kreen](../factions/kreen.md) gate was destroyed.

## Name Note

Canonical spelling per GM ruling (2026-07-31): **Khaz-Tharol** (ruins), **Khaz-Tharol Garrison** (the military location). Transcript drift produced `Khazir`, `Kazir`, `Kazan Throne's ruins` (Session 9), and `Kaz Theril` (the name the [Second Age Bound Spirit](../people/second-age-bound-spirit.md) recognized in Session 4 — evidence the ruins long predate the Seventh Age).

The gate itself was *found* at the [Gorbin Ruins](gorbin-ruins.md) near [Fort Victory](fort-victory.md); it was *destroyed* here at Khaz-Tharol.

## Description

An old stone ruin atop a tall hill, quite rundown outside but professionally garrisoned: hidden snipers in the ruined towers, [tickers](../items/ticker.md) in strategic positions, and magically triggered rock claymores lining the two-wagon-wide approach trail. In Session 3 the party judges it the first truly professional posting they have seen on Driftvale.

## Role in the Campaign

- The party's original post-Hounds orders were a respected, relatively cushy six-month guard posting here at the gate site — arranged by [General Ash](../people/general-ash.md) for his packs before politics redirected them to [Fort Victory](fort-victory.md) ([Session 3](../sessions/session-3.md)).
- [Colonel Dale](../people/colonel-dale.md) commands the garrison and delivered the changed orders.
- Per [Session 9](../sessions/session-9.md), the tracking device [Rurik Valdren](../people/rurik-valdren.md) sought — the key to locating a [Sanctuary City](../concepts/sanctuary-city.md) — was hidden behind a wall near the officer's office here. The party declined the retrieval job; another crew recovered it for Rurik.
