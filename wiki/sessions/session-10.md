# Session 10: The Council, the Contract, and the Justicar's Writ

## Overview

Session 10 brings the party before the [Sunhollow Council](../factions/sunhollow-council.md), converts [Rurik Valdren](../people/rurik-valdren.md)'s intervention into a signed mining agreement, and ends with the arriving Church expedition accepted into [Sunhollow Valley](../places/sunhollow-valley.md) as guests. The session also confirms [Bartholomew Hildebrant](../people/bartholomew-hildebrant.md)'s title as a Warden and gives him a private alliance with [the Justicar](../people/the-justicar.md), including conditional authority to execute [Lord Inquisitor Amir Voss](../people/amir-voss.md) if the Inquisitor crosses into evil.

## The Council and Contract

Before entering the truth-runed council chamber in [Hollowmere](../places/hollowmere.md), the party and Rurik choose exclusive mining rights as the middle course between purchasing the valley and offering only logistical support. The party tells the council about the demon, [Bevar Lurton](../people/bevar-lurton.md) and [Gwen](../people/gwen.md) remaining behind, and the outsiders' teleportation, but does not reveal the living drow or the most dangerous possible meaning of the blood oath's promised release.

Rurik offers transport into and out of the valley, a two-thirds/one-third value split, and a contract registered at the [Radiant Citadel](../places/radiant-citadel.md) to obstruct a Church seizure. The council signs. Ashmane's leonin are the most reluctant, Burrowroot's head is displeased, Briarstep's smaller predator clans wanted a better bargain, and the birdfolk remain comparatively neutral.

[Walter Wifflewick](../people/walter-wifflewick.md) separately contracts with Rurik for a harengon evacuation route if the crisis worsens. The harengon are outside the full blood oath, have stockpiled crystal since learning its value, and may number roughly 80,000 by Rurik's estimate.

## Dreams and the Dead Penitent

[Alistair Hooley](../people/alistair-hooley.md) dreams of walking a burning thread over a void. A voice welcomes him back to his ancestral home while every choice destroys untaken paths. His mother's weaving appears in the imagery, but the sender remains unresolved.

On the night march toward the approaching expedition, the party revisits the dead Penitent scout. The body is preserved by a lethal stasis poison and carries fifteen black arrows made by one hand. The arrows match temple loot, and Bartholomew recognizes the maker's work from his own era. The party removes the arrows, moves the body outside the valley, repositions the beacon to suggest the scout continued onward, and erases its tracks. On the return, Bartholomew identifies the watcher in the cliffs only as the bouncer from the Silk Parlor and [Mistress Selyra Vex'ryn](../people/mistress-selyra-vexryn.md)'s brother; the watcher vanishes when observed.

## The Expedition at the Gate

The Inquisition vanguard includes [Lycus](../people/lycus.md), now captain of the attached mercenary unit, and [Rose](../people/rose.md), his lieutenant. Bartholomew privately gives a hobgoblin caster enough information to warn the Church about the drow, the temple of [Lolth](../people/lolth.md), the demon, and the blood pact.

Amir Voss and Rurik nearly turn the gate meeting into a personal confrontation. The Justicar ends it, accepts the towering Owlin elder's formal hospitality, orders Amir to respect Rurik's lawful standing, and brings the roughly five-hundred-person expedition into the valley as guests. The party and expedition then camp outside [Windcrest Roost](../places/windcrest-roost.md).

## Windcrest and the Warded Tent

Alistair overhears the Owlin briefing the Church. The Owlin identify themselves as the valley's chroniclers, compare old gods with the modern pantheon, and reveal a long effort to understand or escape the blood curse. The conversation also confirms that [Maeve, the Schoolmistress](../people/mave.md) re-ascended as goddess of knowledge, arts, and beauty, while [Belle](../people/belle.md)'s recognition in the current pantheon remains politically contentious.

During a second dream, Bartholomew sees the ruined dwarven civilization and its people transformed into duergar. When asked whether he would surrender his soul or honor to save them, he refuses. The voice answers that his path is set.

The Justicar then summons Bartholomew to a warded tent. He has studied the Wardens and calls Bartholomew his inspiration. Bartholomew reveals that Lolth survives. In return, the Justicar shares the Owlin's 800-year breeding program, the possibility of breaking the blood curse through mass rededication, the Church's history of [Hestia](../people/hestia.md)'s divine fragments, and the assessment that the released demon is the immediate threat.

The Justicar confirms that this is the valley of the [Big Shad](../concepts/big-shad.md), explains how Lolth used demons and the valley's people to convert divine power, and argues for destroying the demon before deciding the temple's fate. He gives Bartholomew a signed writ authorizing the execution of Amir Voss if greed or evil causes Amir to cross the line. He also shows him a dwarf-skin journal recording how [Lucius](../people/lucius.md) waited out the old war and used the transformed dwarves as shock troops.

The recorded session ends after Bartholomew returns with the writ and begins deciding how much of the conversation to share.
