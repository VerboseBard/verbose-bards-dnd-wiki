# Session 11: The Truth-Lantern, the Leonin Plan, and the March to the Demon

## Overview

Session 11 converts the secrets of [Session 10](session-10.md) into consequences. [Bartholomew Hildebrant](../people/bartholomew-hildebrant.md) gives sworn testimony to [Lord Inquisitor Amir Voss](../people/amir-voss.md), the party's trust in him fractures, [Rurik Valdren](../people/rurik-valdren.md) is warned in time to prepare for the seizure of his assets, and [Reggie Norin](../people/reggie-norin.md) brings the party to a secret [Ashmane](../factions/ashmane.md) council that reveals a plan to break the valley's blood curse. The session ends with the coalition inside the demon complex beneath the [Dead Lands](../places/dead-lands.md).

## The Truth-Lantern

The session opens on the aftermath of the warded tent. Reggie and [Jose](../people/jose.md) breached the ward around [the Justicar](../people/the-justicar.md)'s tent and heard the private conversation, including the conditional writ of execution against Amir. The Justicar remains unaware that they heard him.

A scribe separates Bartholomew from the camp and brings him to Amir's tent, which is lit by the [Truth Lantern](../items/truth-lantern.md), under which no lie can be told, nothing can be concealed, and no scrying or invisibility functions. Amir presents dossiers on the party's service record, its concealed intelligence, and payments made to its members, along with allegations against Rurik reaching back to the opening of the gate that released the [Kreen](../factions/kreen.md). Bartholomew gives full signed testimony. He asks about joining the Inquisition; Amir says the Justicars, paladins, or another Church role may be a better fit.

Amir explains that the [Inquisition](../factions/inquisition.md) exists to hunt demons and to find evil concealed inside righteous institutions. Inquisitors watch Justicars as Justicars watch other institutions, and every watcher signs a writ of execution against himself, entrusted to a Justicar. Amir's own signature is on the [writ Bartholomew carries](../items/amir-voss-execution-writ.md). He also gives his account of the last war's causes: no Justicar oversaw the Inquisition posted to Rurik, no one held the gods accountable before the [War of the Gods](../concepts/war-of-the-gods.md), and the [Divine Gate](../concepts/divine-gate.md) was down. He describes a postwar world that lost divine government faster than mortal institutions could replace it, and regards the valley's ether as the leverage the Church now lacks.

## A Divided Party

When Bartholomew returns, Jose strikes him and the party demands an account. Bartholomew describes most of the Justicar conversation but conceals that he signed a complete statement and understates what will follow for Rurik. [Sergeant Jefferson Stone](../people/sergeant-jefferson-stone.md) tells him the party will cooperate against the demon and the drow but no longer trusts him otherwise. Four Inquisitors ward the tent from outside during the argument, and the party is escorted to the Church's side of the camp.

Travelling from [Windcrest Roost](../places/windcrest-roost.md) to [Hollowmere](../places/hollowmere.md), [Rose](../people/rose.md) returns the sealed dispatch container, opened. [Lycus](../people/lycus.md) removed the material concerning the party before the report left the valley. The party's names are absent from the record, but they are recorded as not to be trusted and will receive no further Church work.

## Warnings and Contingencies

The party warns Rurik. He learns that the Inquisition is moving to seize his assets outside the valley and to take his contract by legal process. Rather than contest the charge, he prepares: he implants a sequence of memories in Jefferson containing a route through the [Underdark](../places/underdark-upside-down.md) to [Sanctuary City](../concepts/sanctuary-city.md), the combination to his safe at the [Radiant Citadel](../places/radiant-citadel.md), and contacts to approach if the banking system closes to him. The memories are fogged so they can only be recalled in order and cannot be taken by mental probing. He asks that if he cannot reach the dwarves held there, Jefferson will.

Rurik also divides his personnel, sending most to relocate the teleportation circle to the valley's edge for [Walter Wifflewick](../people/walter-wifflewick.md)'s harengon evacuation while keeping a small guard with him. The evacuation is under way but no portal transport has yet been completed. [Alistair Hooley](../people/alistair-hooley.md) learns that the harengon now regard him as an heir of the old line returned through the fog; altars are being raised in his name and competitions have begun for his hand.

## The Ashmane Council

Reggie brings the party to twelve leonin of the Ashmane, none of them the council members the party has met. Their elder explains that bringing the matter to the full [Sunhollow Council](../factions/sunhollow-council.md) would have caused immediate civil war.

He gives the clan's history. Humans enslaved the leonin roughly fourteen hundred years ago on an island off the continent. Four centuries later an ancestor who had escaped returned and raised a rebellion that nearly succeeded, but the allies who had promised aid never came; the survivors were sold to a drow, and the bloodline enslavement descends from that sale. The elder is a descendant of people earlier heroes promised to help and did not.

Because the curse was built for elven and humanoid blood, the [Longbough](../factions/longbough.md) Hadozee are bound most deeply and can least resist it. The leonin expect the Hadozee accompanying the expedition to be compelled to attack once the demon is dead and accept that those attackers may die. They ask the party to warn only a small Church command group and to ensure that any reprisal does not extend to the wider Hadozee people.

Their [curse-breaking plan](../concepts/leonin-curse-breaking-ritual.md) is to seize the valley's defensive matrix and use its interface to hijack the drow ritual and break the curse for every bound people at once. The demon must be destroyed or its corruption purified first. Success is not assured, and the cost depends on how far the ritual proceeds before any interruption: interrupted early, everyone in the valley could die; further on, roughly half; later still, chiefly the leonin who began it. The ritual will consume every ether crystal connected to the valley. Rurik is told this price. Amir and the Justicar are not. The party considers sheltering reserve crystals in its [Bag of Holding](../items/squad-bag-of-holding.md), but neither their survival nor the failure timing of active crystal devices is established.

The elder also recounts that the drow who bought his people arrived on a great wyvern, that records show it was tended for half a generation until its keepers stopped returning, and that it has not been seen in a thousand years. He assumes it is held in stasis somewhere in the valley.

Before the march, Bartholomew is offered his choice of the dwarf-leather book or rest. He sleeps, and remembers the planning of the original operation: thirteen tables, each assigned a temple, and the Justicar's report that nine were destroyed, with two more never accounted for. Those figures account for only twelve sites when Sunhollow is included, leaving one site unexplained.

## The March and the Hadozee Contingent

The coalition forms from Church forces, Inquisitors, mercenaries, [Briarstep](../factions/briarstep.md) fighters, [Windcrest](../factions/windcrest.md) scouts, a few Ashmane, and a Hadozee contingent of twenty casters and forty fighters with a council head attending. The harengon are largely absent, their evacuation having begun. The party arms trusted leonin champions with crystal-powered weapons.

Amir carries the lantern at the head of the column, burning back the corruption as he goes. At the ruined village the ghost of the elven forest keeper appears; Amir releases her from her suffering, and the Hadozee council head rebukes him for destroying a guardian that had kept the path clear. Passing through the tar and blighted ground costs several of the party Constitution and reduces the effect of healing on them. The party leads the column away from the road to [Ssar'Velyn Temple](../places/ssar-velyn-temple.md), and the watchers in the webs draw back as they turn aside.

Near the tunnel mouth Amir contacts Jefferson by thought, says the demon is stronger than expected, and proposes dealing with the Hadozee before the anticipated ambush. Jefferson agrees. He and Reggie move the [Burrowroot](../factions/burrowroot.md) clear of the formation. The council head makes eye contact with Jefferson before the Hadozee, already boxed within the column, are fired on by Church casters, soldiers, and mercenaries. The contingent and its council head are killed in seconds, before any of them has acted. [Penelope Puckle](../people/penelope-puckle.md), crying, recovers the council head's staff and the trappings of his office. [Captain Havlin](../people/captain-havlin.md) carries out a fallen member of his own escort, named as Darien. At Jefferson's insistence the dead are gathered and burned under control. Rose observes that they had not yet become traitors. The drider the party once fought as a champion watches from the webbing and withdraws.

## The Demon Complex

Inside, the Justicar kneels, reads the ground, and reports two demons rather than one. He takes the stronger; Amir and his five Inquisitors take the lesser. Both manifest wings as they raise their power, and Amir draws additionally on his bodyguard. The party chooses to follow the Justicar, in part to keep him from spending the divine spark that would kill him.

The chamber holds a vast [demon-draining engine](../concepts/demon-draining-engine.md): an altar and broken chains built to drain a demon of the divine power it had absorbed and convert it into a form mortals could use. The chains have been broken only a week or two. [Alistair](../people/alistair-hooley.md) raises a crystal for light, sees thousands of bound souls layered over the room, and reads ritual script written in an old elven tongue across the walls and floor. The knowledge locks into him, costing him [sanity](../concepts/sanity.md), and a forty-foot demon manifests on the altar, tethered to him by a cord from his chest.

Alistair fails to sever the link and then wins the contest of wills, and both he and the demon bleed. He breaks line of sight behind a pillar at further cost, and the tether holds. As the surrounding fiends fade from his sight, they become solid to the rest of the party. The recording ends before initiative.
