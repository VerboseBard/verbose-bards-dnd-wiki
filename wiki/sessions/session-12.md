# Session 12: The Demon Falls and the Tether Remains

## Overview

The coalition defeats both demons beneath the [Dead Lands](../places/dead-lands.md), and [the Justicar](../people/the-justicar.md) survives to restore the fallen on the other front. [Alistair Hooley](../people/alistair-hooley.md) helps turn the battle through the connection that traps him outside ordinary reality, but the demon's destruction does not end his tether. In the aftermath, the party separates: [Bartholomew Hildebrant](../people/bartholomew-hildebrant.md) remains with the Church, while the others accompany [Rurik Valdren](../people/rurik-valdren.md) toward his portal and then prepare to return to the valley's people. The session ends with the Church's cleansing still under way and the [leonin curse-breaking ritual](../concepts/leonin-curse-breaking-ritual.md) unresolved.

Source: [Session 12 recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eqYr6r-jOVk), with a [reviewed automated transcript](session-12-transcript.md). The recording runs 4:58:14.

## Between Two Realities

Alistair's body stands defenseless inside the [demon-draining engine](../concepts/demon-draining-engine.md), while his awareness confronts the greater demon through the cord attached to his chest. The other combatants appear distant or ghostlike to him. Attempts to weaken the cord cause him pain; the connection loosens but does not break. Alistair loses several [sanity](../concepts/sanity.md) points over the course of the battle with the demon.

The Justicar empowers allied weapons with radiant force and takes the greater demon head-on. Rurik is caught, bitten and thrown, but survives and returns to the fight. [Jefferson Stone](../people/sergeant-jefferson-stone.md), [Jose](../people/jose.md), Bartholomew and their allies fight through waves of lesser demons and corrupted spiders. [Reggie Norin](../people/reggie-norin.md) protects and heals Alistair's exposed body. [Penelope Puckle](../people/penelope-puckle.md) uses her gifted pistol despite her unfamiliarity with the weapon, and Jose's drone helps disrupt enemies preparing attacks.

Alistair discovers that his strange position gives him influence over the fight. His taunting distracts the demon, creating an opening for the Justicar. Later, he draws a chalk unicorn across part of the chamber's spellwork. The altered inscription closes one reinforcement passage, cutting off creatures crossing through it, but makes the tether more firmly attached to him. He then turns the demon's own pull against it, causing it to buckle and vomit blood while the others continue fighting.

## Victory Without the Sacrifice

The Justicar releases a line of radiant power that destroys two nearby ogre-like demons and disintegrates the greater demon. Alistair returns to ordinary awareness as the cord shrinks to about the width of a finger. It appears to fade, but it has not gone.

The remaining enemies are put down and the Justicar immediately hurries toward the other chamber. The lesser demon there is dead, but several Inquisitors have suffered terrible injuries. Because the Justicar has survived with his restoration powers available, the fallen can be brought back. The coalition suffers no additional permanent deaths in this battle; the earlier killing of the Hadozee contingent remains a separate and unreversed loss.

[Amir Voss](../people/amir-voss.md) survives and greets Bartholomew with satisfaction. His intense expression of triumph is disturbing to observe, but the scene supplies no ruling that he has become corrupt and no execution follows. He warns that news of the Hadozee killing could make the return dangerous and worries about the valley's defensive systems being turned against the expedition.

Inspection suggests that the lesser demon broke free more recently than the greater—perhaps three or four days earlier. Its comparatively low surrounding corruption raises questions about how long it was present on this plane. Neither its arrival nor the identity of whoever broke either set of chains is settled.

## Blood, Books and a Lingering Cord

Church personnel place sacred relics around the facility to prepare its purification. Jefferson investigates a vat of unusual liquid. The hearth presence associated with [Hestia](../people/hestia.md) describes it as refined demon blood mixed with divine energy and crystals. Jefferson accepts the presence's continuing help while refusing unquestioning service. She recognizes the family he has made of his companions and observes that a parent's care does not guarantee every child's goodness.

Jefferson bottles a [sample of the refined mixture](../items/refined-demon-blood-mixture.md). Contact strips flesh from his hand, which grows back as his nanites react. Some nanites are destroyed and others enhanced. This is a different substance and recovery from the [raw demonic ichor](../items/demonic-ichor-vial.md) collected earlier inside the temple.

Before the Church gathers the complex's books, Jose finds an old [treatise on Underdark monsters and flora](../items/underdark-field-treatise.md) with pages deliberately torn out. The party also recovers three ornate elven books, while Rurik removes a smaller book concealed inside another volume. Their full contents remain unread.

Outside, Rurik briefly makes Alistair's remaining tether visible. He warns that managing such a connection might lead to power, while mishandling it could cost Alistair his existence. This is a warning about an unresolved bond, not proof that the demon is controlling him.

## Two Departures

Bartholomew stays to rest, assist and guard the Church during the cleansing. The other party members leave with Rurik. Most of their beastfolk allies remain behind for a long rest before following.

Rurik warns that his legal struggle may keep the party from accessing banked money for a year. He also fears that the harengon evacuation will not finish before the curse-breaking ritual consumes the valley's ether. He discusses Unity's guild lands as the evacuation destination and the difficulties facing those who reach them, but no completed population count is established.

A familiar drider shadows the travellers without attacking. An unnamed drow woman associated with the casino steps out of the darkness and walks beside them. She offers help recovering the property left with [Bevar Lurton](../people/bevar-lurton.md), asks Rurik to leave his portal available, and gives the party a [sending ring](../items/drow-sending-ring.md) for future contact.

She also claims that the slain Hadozee included the lineages carrying much of their people's magical inheritance, setting future magical development back for generations except where surviving children can carry it on. Her account adds a possible generational cost to the killing; it remains the drow woman's claim rather than an independently verified census or law of inheritance.

At the portal, the travellers find the recovered bag and its contents. Jose senses that the bag has changed, though its storage still works. Rurik repeats his offer of a substantial reward for following the protected memories he entrusted to Jefferson and helping the dwarves in [Sanctuary City](../concepts/sanctuary-city.md). He then passes through the portal, exhausting the crystals available for transport. The portal remains in place but needs more power before anyone else can pass.

## Bartholomew's Unfinished History

While the others travel, Bartholomew revisits his earlier service in dwarven special forces, before he worked with the mixed-race group. His unit brought down a cult that chained demons and consumed their blood, distorting its members and opening them to possession. Among the dead cultists were firstborn children of powerful dwarven families.

In a meeting months later, the head of his order praises the raid but explains its political cost. He suspects that the families supplied the resources for their children's involvement, although he cannot prove it. Influential people favor pursuing refined demonic power, while a growing military faction wants to combine it with undead power to build an army against the undead hordes. To move Bartholomew's team out of that political pressure, the order reassigns them to a special branch working with elves, halflings, and other peoples. Their demon-hunting work continues; this is how his earlier dwarven service leads into the mixed-race assignment.

He then reads the [dwarven survivor's journal](../items/dwarven-survivors-journal.md). Its narrator describes a dwarven pocket realm cut off from other worlds, shortages exploited by powerful families and a demonic military force called the Legion of the Dead or the Damned. The journal connects the faction opposed in Bartholomew's earlier life with a regime that rose in the Fifth Age. It recounts immense groups of civilians disappearing into rituals and a sacrifice involving approximately 250,000 people. The narrator survives a passage through reality because of her bloodline and reaches an unidentified world.

The survivor's account has been attached to an older ritual book. Bartholomew recognizes that underlying volume from the cult site in his recovered memory. A presence presses against his mind when he opens it, and he closes the book. The narrator's identity, destination and the book's route back into the present remain unknown.

## By the River

The Justicar prepares to begin several more hours of cleansing while Bartholomew watches the entrance. The others choose to rest beside the river and gather crystals before returning, rather than exhausting themselves in a forced march.

Identification of the drow ring reveals both its sending function and a periodic location signal. Investigation of the recovered second bag reveals something stranger: it is a living creature with dimensional storage, called a [Bag of Devour](../items/living-storage-bag.md) by the GM. Its precise nature and handling require further research. The discovery does not turn the party's original [Squad Bag of Holding](../items/squad-bag-of-holding.md) into the same creature.

The valley's curse, the fate of its crystal reserves, the evacuation's final numbers and the future of its concealment all remain unresolved at the recording's end.

## Later Continuity

[Session 13](session-13.md) resolves the ritual's immediate result: the curse-breaking ritual succeeds, connected valley crystals are consumed, and all Driftvale fog disperses. It also resolves the storage distinction: crystals in the ordinary Bag of Holding survive, while crystals in the living bag do not. The final evacuation, casualty, ritualist, temple-purge, and tether outcomes remain open.
