# Session 13: By the Skin of Their Teeth

## Overview

Session 13 closes the immediate Sunhollow operation without resolving every consequence. The party chooses to begin the [Leonin Curse-Breaking Ritual](../concepts/leonin-curse-breaking-ritual.md) early, preventing the expected compelled Hadozee civilian host from reaching the battle. At [Hollowmere](../places/hollowmere.md), the defenders survive a seven-round assault by the narrowest possible margin. On the Church front, [Bartholomew Hildebrant](../people/bartholomew-hildebrant.md) recovers [Amir Voss's execution writ](../items/amir-voss-execution-writ.md), but [Lord Inquisitor Amir Voss](../people/amir-voss.md) is separately killed by Selyra's escaping archer brother. The [Justicar](../people/the-justicar.md) kills [Mistress Selyra Vex'ryn](../people/mistress-selyra-vexryn.md) after Bartholomew destroys her portal.

The GM awards full ritual completion: the valley's connected ether crystals are consumed and all [Driftvale](../places/driftvale.md) fog disperses. That is a confirmed victory, not a completed aftermath ledger. Refugee totals, the complete Church and leonin casualty count, the ritualists' final condition, surviving crystal inventory, and the temple purge remain unresolved.

Source: [The 7th Age Session 13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=URP7RmYTAX8), approximately 6:38. The [reviewed automated transcript](session-13-transcript.md) preserves the recording's table discussion while separating transcript repairs from setting canon.

## The Early Start

The coalition enters Session 13 weakened by the earlier preemptive killing of the Hadozee contingent. Some elders withdraw support, and the leonin report a much larger, potentially compelled Hadozee gathering about twelve hours away. [Reggie Norin](../people/reggie-norin.md) argues to start immediately rather than wait for civilians to become a battlefield threat. The party accepts that risk, organizes harassment and withdrawal teams, demolishes approaches while leaving one predictable bridge, and prepares barricades, honey, caltrops, oil, ball bearings, and explosive charges.

[Eric Vossel](../people/eric-vossel.md), Rurik's aide, offers either combat support or a faster evacuation rate. The party sends him to the evacuation work. No final evacuation total is narrated, and the Ticker is discussed but not handed over.

## The Church Front: Wyvern, Writ, and Archer

When the ritual announces itself, the Church realizes it cannot return in time and turns toward [Ssar'Velyn Temple](../places/ssar-velyn-temple.md). A huge wyvern rises carrying enemy forces. With support from Amir and other Church members, the Justicar destroys its wing and crashes it, preventing planned reinforcements from reaching Hollowmere. The Justicar later kills the wounded wyvern.

At the crash, Bartholomew sees the missing execution writ in the hand of an armored enemy paladin. He uses a combat ruling to seize it, briefly loses it again, then recovers and secures it before it can be activated. The party does not learn who originally transferred the writ to the enemy or why. The same paladin later tries to use a Meteor Swarm scroll; Bartholomew takes that as well, and allied rifle fire kills the fleeing paladin.

The archer whose distinctive black arrows Bartholomew recognized in earlier sessions separately poisons and disintegrates Amir. The [Truth Lantern](../items/truth-lantern.md) falls and shatters. The writ did not cause Amir's death. The archer escapes through the darkness; player-facing canon identifies him only as Selyra's brother and the archer, not by a private name.

## Hollowmere's Defense

The Hollowmere team must protect the ritual through round fourteen, with the GM allowing a complete defeat of the assault force to secure the remaining unopposed time. The first crisis comes from the high-elf mental commander compelling [Captain Sgt. Jose](../people/jose.md) to break into the ritual room. [Reggie](../people/reggie-norin.md) redirects Jose long enough to prevent an immediate breach, and the party's prepared traps and fire weaken attackers before they reach the final line.

The fight remains close. The commander later controls an unnamed leonin gate guard, who carries one of the prepared charges into the ritual door. The blast destroys the door and downs multiple allies, but an open route is not itself an effective interruption. The commander misses her psychic-burst recharge, then fails to land the needed follow-up attack on the ritual.

In round seven, [Alistair Hooley](../people/alistair-hooley.md) gives [Sgt. Jefferson Stone](../people/sergeant-jefferson-stone.md) the movement needed to reach the commander. Jose forces the commander to spend her reaction, Jefferson ignites her, and [Penelope Puckle](../people/penelope-puckle.md) kills her with forty damage against thirty-nine remaining hit points. A surviving leonin then shoots the last echo knight. One point less damage would have let the commander act and disrupt the ceremony, with the stated consequence of roughly half the valley dying.

The GM's final defender roll call records four ritual-room survivors: Captain Havlin, Penelope, Reggie, and one leonin fighter. Havlin was not the compelled bomber. Reggie is restored only after combat ends. The broader leonin and Church casualty totals are not recorded.

## The Portal and the Fog

The rapid Church-side victory gives Bartholomew one final chance to reach the temple portal. He shoots its power crystal; an edge-case ruling causes a chain explosion that destroys the portal and throws Selyra back. The Justicar follows with the narrated killing strike. Selyra is dead, while her archer brother remains at large. The temple itself has not yet been purged, and the status of [Bevar Lurton](../people/bevar-lurton.md), [Gwen](../people/gwen.md), other occupants, and a vague reference to a bag with a head priestess remains unresolved.

The ritual then finishes. Connected valley crystals go dark, all Driftvale fog disperses, and weapons outside the valley continue working. The last table ruling distinguishes storage: crystals in the party's ordinary [Squad Bag of Holding](../items/squad-bag-of-holding.md) survive, while crystals inside the [Living Storage Bag](../items/living-storage-bag.md) do not. Exact counts still require inventory reconciliation.

## Session 14 Position

The declared next session is the temple purge and ritual aftermath. Bartholomew and the surviving Church members are at the temple; Jefferson, Alistair, Jose, and surviving local allies remain at Hollowmere. The party has not yet reunited on the recording.
